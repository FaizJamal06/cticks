# cTicks website copy

The landing page at `/`, the short invite page at `/?join=CODE`, and the link-preview text. Every claim here is checked against the app at the bottom of this file. House rules: short, casual, Indian context, no em dashes, no fake numbers, testimonials or logos.

**Name:** cTicks (lowercase c, capital T). "Ticks" means two things, and the copy uses both: the ✓ you tick off every day, and the clock ticking toward midnight.

**Tagline:** "Tick it or pay for it." (alternatives are on the /brand page).

**Cast:** two made-up friends. **Aryaan** is "you" (we see his phone). **Shanayaa** is his friend.
- Aryaan's tasks: Gym · 2 LeetCode problems · No reels before noon
- Shanayaa's tasks: Run 5k · Read 20 pages · Practice guitar
- Settings: round 1, 7 days (Mon to Sun), ₹100 per missed day, a miss = leaving any task undone ("Finish every task")

**The week:** Mon and Tue clean for both. Wednesday is the big day: Shanayaa ticks first, nudges Aryaan, he gets the 9pm warning and still skips the gym, so Wednesday is a miss. Shanayaa misses Friday, Aryaan misses Saturday. Final score: Aryaan 2 misses, Shanayaa 1, so Aryaan owes Shanayaa ₹100.

## How to install

**H2:** Put it on your home screen.

**Intro:** cTicks is a web app. No app store, nothing to download. Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app and can send you notifications.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open cTicks in Chrome.
2. Tap **Install** when it pops up. No pop-up? Tap **⋮**, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
3. Open it from your home screen and turn on notifications.

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Open cTicks in Safari.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the new icon and turn on notifications.

**Note:** On iPhone, notifications only work once cTicks is on your Home Screen. That's Apple's rule. You need iOS 16.4 or newer.

**Note:** Opened the link inside WhatsApp or Instagram? Open it in Chrome or Safari first. The app's built-in browser can't install anything.

## FAQ

**Is it free?**
Yes. No ads, no subscription.

**What's Pay or Dare?**
Miss a day and you choose: pay the stake to each friend, or spin a dare. Do it, send the proof to your group chat, and the group votes in the app. Paying is always an option.

**Who sees my dare video?**
Only the people you send it to. cTicks never sees, uploads or stores videos or photos. Proof goes to your group chat. cTicks holds the vote.

**Can I get kicked out?**
In Pay or Dare, yes: ignore a penalty for 48 hours, fail a dare twice and then don't pay, or miss 3 days in a row. You can rejoin when the next round starts.

**Are the dares safe?**
They're meant to be silly, not risky: sing a chorus, a 30-second dance. Custom dares need a second person's OK, and anything dangerous, illegal or public is off limits. Anyone can report a dare.

**Can we just play for money?**
Yes. Pick Money only when you start a challenge, or switch in settings from the next round.

**Does cTicks move money?**
No. It keeps score and tells you who owes whom. You pay on UPI or however you like.

**Can you cheat?**
Not easily. You can only tick your own tasks, and only today's. "Today" comes from our server in India time, so changing your phone's clock does nothing. If someone edits their task list, everyone gets told.

**Does it work on iPhone?**
Yes, on iOS 16.4 or newer, once you add it to your Home Screen from Safari. Older iPhones can still use it, just without notifications.

**How many people can play?**
2 to 6 per challenge. A missed day costs the stake to every other player, unless you take a dare and it passes.

**Does it work on a laptop?**
Yes, in any modern browser. Notifications work there too.

**When does a day end?**
At midnight, India time. The warning comes around 9pm. Results, new penalties and every Pay or Dare deadline land at 9am.
